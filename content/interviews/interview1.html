Ivo Brouwer is a graphic artist , calligrapher & type designer based in The Hague, Netherlands. His work spans expressive typefaces, lettering, patterns, and writing tools and exists in books, musea, galleries and public space. Next to that, he is also part of the collective High on Type.

We didn’t do the best jm meeting with Ivo, but luckily, he was patient with us and we managed to make it work. We spoke to him shortly after High on Type published the alphabet zine from their residency at My Monkey Gallery in Nancy, so we were excited to hear more about how it came together. During the talk, we also learned about collective practice and whether any practice can ever be truly individuob organizing our Zooal.
