Céline Hurka is a type designer based in The Hague, the Netherlands. In her practice, she often uses the word "Collaboration," which makes her the perfect interview partner for us. Besides building a strong network by showing up and taking care of relationships, she also tries to get her students to collaborate with each other and preparing them for the real design world. By freeing up time elsewhere and not being on social media, she could give that time to us and talk with us. Through her pop-cultural analogies and her stories about friendships, the longest of our interviews developed.

Céline
