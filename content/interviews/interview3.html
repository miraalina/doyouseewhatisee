Fabian Harb is a graphic designer based in Basel, Switzerland – we reached him from Bremen while he was in Portugal. Together with Johannes Breyer, he directs the digital type foundry Dinamo. Fabian was our first interviewee, who helped us to get rid of our anxiety about interviews – even when connection issues meant we had to do the conversation without cameras. He gave us insights into the behind-the-scenes at Dinamo, the benefits of sharing work, explaining how he and Johannes figured out how to create a foundry together and run it, even while working across distances!
