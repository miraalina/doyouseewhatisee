Information

The 26 lowercase characters are each crafted as a continuous closed loop, using twists to build out the anatomy of every letter. The typeface includes filled and outlined variations to explore the relationship between form and stroke.

Form-finding

After our experiment, we noticed several recurring patterns in our sketches: quirky ornaments, textural strokes, and parallel lines. However, we were most captivated by a few sketches that explored an elusive interaction between stroke and form, where counters and letter elements emerged from looping paths rather than distinct shapes. Rather than simply recreating the sketches, we extracted this principle to build an entirely new alphabet.

Digitization

Each letter was digitally drawn from a single continuous loop. Both the filled and outlined versions share the exact same underlying stroke.
