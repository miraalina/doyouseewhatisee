Information

The letterset consists of 26 lowercase letters designed in the style of a typewriter typeface.

Form-finding

After experimenting with different approaches, we decided to develop the letterset on a grid. Using the rounded shape of the sponge the letters are built from a multitude of individual black dots placed closely together, creating a distinct textured appearance. We moved away from the idea of "hacking" the tool and instead focused on more classical letterforms. The stroke contrast commonly found in calligraphy is mimicked through the overlapping of individual dots, particularly in areas such as the descenders.

Digitization

After scanning the drawing sheets, we processed the scans in Photoshop to refine the stamped shapes, adjusting their contrast and depth while preserving the irregular, grainy texture characteristic of a handmade stamp impression. The forms were then vectorized in Adobe Illustrator and imported into Glyphs. Finally, we made small adjustments to the letter heights and proportions to achieve a more consistent typeface.
