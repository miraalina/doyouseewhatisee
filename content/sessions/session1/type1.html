Information

The font features a set of monolinear handwritten characters, including 26 uppercase letters, 26 lowercase letters, and numerals, along with a stylistic alternate set that showcases different interpretations of the alphabet.

Form-finding

The letters are a fusion of our two individual handwritings. On the one hand, characteristic features of both handwritings were combined to create new letterforms. On the other hand, different components of existing letters were assembled in a "Frankenstein-like" way to construct new forms. Both approaches exist side by side as stylistic alternates, offering two different interpretations of the same alphabet.

Digitization

After scanning the drawing sheets and scaling the letters for uniformity, only the core skeleton of each character was traced rather than its outer appearance. The final weight was then applied directly through the stroke thickness.
