BEYOND LATIN

During "Negotiating Type", a workshop about collaboratively working on type lead by Katharina Nejdl, I came to two realizations: First: Democratizing design without communication leads to misalignment. We can’t work toward a common goal if we don’t know what the other is thinking. Second: We often reference conventional forms of Latin letters, which restricts our ability to explore and develop new forms. How can we take advantage of miscommunications during collaborations into the process of ideation? How can we explore unexpected forms of latin characters?
