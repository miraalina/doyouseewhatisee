Information

The typeface consists of 26 lowercase letters constructed from geometric shapes on a square grid. Strict rules define the construction of each letter, intentionally allowing for a certain degree of reduced legibility.

Form-finding

Following the strict rules established through the decisions made by the tools, the form, number, and arrangement of the shapes were largely predetermined. In our case, the parameters were that all shapes had to be identical in size, not touch each other, and maintain equal spacing. Our main creative decision was to vary the arrangement of the shapes within these given parameters. This restricted approach to form-finding became an essential part of the design process.

Digitization

The individual letters were digitized in Glyphs. First, the dimensions of the square were defined, which then served as the basis for determining the dimensions of the other shapes and the spacing between them. The letters were then reconstructed digitally according to these measurements and rules.
