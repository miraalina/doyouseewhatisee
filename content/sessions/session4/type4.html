Information

Drawn using a flat brush, the 26 uppercase letters each required at least two people to produce, making the physical tension between the participants an active part of the design process.

Form-finding

The form-finding process was straightforward, following the rules of our exercise: one person drew while the other shifted the paper below. We then curated the final character set based on legibility, compelling form, and visual harmony across the system.

Digitization

After scanning the drawing sheets, the letters were extracted and scaled to fit the system. They were then image-traced to faithfully capture the distinct texture of the original tool.
